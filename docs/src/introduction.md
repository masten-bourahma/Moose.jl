# Introduction 

### Context
Astronomy entered the big data era!, many instruments are now providing millions of spectra. Automatically calssifying these spectra into objects (starm galaxy, QSO) and predicting their redshift 
requires an automated processing and analysis. `Moose.jl` aims at providing automaticity in the redshift prediction task.

### The Multi-Unit Spectroscopic Explorer
The Multi-Unit Spectroscopic Explorer (MUSE), is an Integral Field Spectrograph at the Very Large Telescope in Chili. MUSE provides hyperspectral observations covering the visible wavelengths ($$\sim 4500 \AA - 9350 \AA$$), .

 
 MUSE is used to study galaxies and their environment, by taking deep exposures of galaxy fields ()

 and can observe very faint objects (up to $$\textrm{magF775W} \sim 32$$)
